How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Sex, Height, feet and Height, inches.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Devine formula: 50 kg for men (45.5 kg for women) + 2.3 kg for each inch of height over 5 feet.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Sex: Male; Height, feet: 5; Height, inches: 9), the result is Ideal weight 156 lbs. It also shows In kilograms: 70.7 kg; Healthy BMI range for this height: 125 to 169 lbs.

Good to know

The formula was created for drug dosing, not as a goal weight. The healthy BMI range is a better guide.
