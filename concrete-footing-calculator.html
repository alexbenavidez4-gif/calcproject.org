The formula

Volume = length (ft) × width (in) ÷ 12 × depth (in) ÷ 12. Divide by 27 for cubic yards.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Footing length: 40 ft; Footing width: 16 in; Footing depth: 8 in), the result is Cubic yards 1.32 cu yd. It also shows Volume: 35.6 cu ft; 80 lb bags: 60 bags.

Good to know

  • Footing size is set by your local building code and soil. A common rule is twice the wall thickness wide and as deep as the wall is thick.
  • Most residential footings get two continuous runs of #4 rebar. Check your local code.
  • Dig to undisturbed soil. Never pour a footing on loose fill.

Common questions

How wide should a concrete footing be?

A common rule is twice the width of the wall it carries: 16 inches for an 8 inch block wall. Poor soil or heavy loads need wider footings.

How deep should a footing be?

The bottom of the footing must be below the local frost line. The footing itself is usually 8 to 12 inches thick.

How much concrete is in a footing?

Multiply length by width by depth, all in feet, and divide by 27. A 40 ft footing that is 16 inches wide and 8 inches deep takes 1.32 cubic yards.
