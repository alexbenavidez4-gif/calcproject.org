The formula

Geometric mean = the nth root of the product of n numbers.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Numbers, separated by commas or spaces: 2, 8, 32), the result is Geometric mean 8. It also shows Arithmetic mean: 14.

Good to know

Use it to average growth rates and ratios. It is always less than or equal to the ordinary average.
