How to use the deck boards calculator

  1. Measure the deck length and width in feet.
  2. Enter the actual board width. A 5/4x6 or 2x6 deck board is 5.5 inches wide.
  3. Enter the gap between boards. One eighth of an inch is typical.
  4. Enter the board length you will buy and a waste allowance.

The formula

Deck area ÷ (board width + gap) gives total board length, plus waste, divided by board length.

Worked example

For example, a 16×12 ft deck needs about 35 boards at 12 ft each.

Good to know

  • Choose a board length that matches the deck width so you avoid butt joints.
  • Diagonal decking needs about 15 percent waste.
  • This covers the walking surface only. Joists, beams, posts, railings, and stairs are extra.

Common questions

How many deck boards do I need for a 12x16 deck?

A 12 × 16 ft deck is 192 square feet. With 5.5 inch boards, a 1/8 inch gap, and 10 percent waste you need about 451 linear feet, or 38 boards that are 12 ft long.

How wide is a 5/4x6 deck board?

The actual size is 1 inch thick and 5.5 inches wide.

How much gap should I leave between deck boards?

About 1/8 inch for dry or composite boards. Wet pressure-treated boards can be installed tight because they shrink as they dry.
