How to use the siding calculator

  1. Walk the house and add the length of every outside wall for the perimeter.
  2. Enter the average wall height from the foundation to the soffit.
  3. Count the doors and windows.
  4. Enter the coverage per panel from the product label, and a waste allowance of 10 to 15 percent.

The formula

Perimeter × wall height, minus door and window area, plus waste, divided by panel coverage.

Worked example

For example, a 160 linear ft home with 9 ft walls, two doors, and eight windows needs about 92 panels at 16 sq ft each.

Good to know

  • Gable ends are triangles. Add half of the gable width times its height to your area.
  • Siding is often sold by the square, which is 100 square feet.
  • You also need starter strip, J-channel, corner posts, and trim. These are sold by the linear foot.

Common questions

How do I calculate how much siding I need?

Multiply the house perimeter by the wall height, subtract doors and windows, add gables, then add 10 to 15 percent for waste.

What is a square of siding?

One square covers 100 square feet of wall. A house with 1,500 square feet of wall needs 15 squares before waste.

How much siding waste should I allow?

Ten percent for a simple box-shaped house and 15 percent for houses with many gables, dormers, and windows.
