The formula

Divide both numbers by their greatest common factor. 18 : 24 has a greatest common factor of 6, so it simplifies to 3 : 4.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (First number: 18; Second number: 24), the result is Simplified ratio 3 : 4. It also shows As a decimal: 0.75; First number as a share of the total: 42.86%.

Good to know

A ratio compares two amounts. A ratio of 3 : 4 means 3 parts of one thing for every 4 parts of the other.
