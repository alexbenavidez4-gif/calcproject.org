How to use the raised bed soil calculator

  1. Measure the inside length and width of the bed in feet.
  2. Enter how deep you will fill it, in inches. Stop an inch or two below the rim.
  3. Enter how many beds of that size you have and the bag size you plan to buy.

The formula

Soil = bed length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 × number of beds, in cubic feet. Divide by 27 for cubic yards or by the bag size for bags.

Worked example

One 8 × 4 ft bed filled 12 inches deep needs 32 cubic feet of soil. That is 1.19 cubic yards, or 22 bags of 1.5 cubic foot raised bed mix.

Good to know

  • A common fill is about 60 percent topsoil, 30 percent compost, and 10 percent aeration material such as perlite or coarse sand.
  • Soil settles 10 to 15 percent in the first season. Plan to top up.
  • For three or more beds, bulk delivery of garden mix usually costs much less than bags.

Common questions

How much soil do I need for a 4x8 raised bed?

At 12 inches deep, 32 cubic feet (1.19 cubic yards). At 6 inches deep, 16 cubic feet.

How deep should raised bed soil be?

Six to 8 inches is enough for lettuce and herbs. Twelve inches suits most vegetables, and 18 inches is better for carrots and other deep roots.

How many bags of soil fill a raised bed?

Divide the cubic feet by the bag size. A 32 cubic foot bed takes 22 bags of 1.5 cubic feet or 16 bags of 2 cubic feet.
