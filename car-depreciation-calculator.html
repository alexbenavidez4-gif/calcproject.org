How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Purchase price, Years owned, First-year drop and Drop each year after.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Value = price × (1 − first-year drop) × (1 − yearly drop)years − 1.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Purchase price: 35000; Years owned: 5; First-year drop: 20; Drop each year after: 15), the result is Estimated value $14,616.17. It also shows Value lost: $20,383.83; Share of value lost: 58.2%.

Good to know

New cars commonly lose around 20 percent in the first year and 15 percent a year after, but it varies a lot by model and mileage.
