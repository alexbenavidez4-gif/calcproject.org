How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in First day of last period and Average cycle length.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Naegele’s rule: due date = first day of the last period + 280 days, adjusted for cycles longer or shorter than 28 days.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (First day of last period: 2026-01-01; Average cycle length: 28), the result is Estimated due date Thursday, October 8, 2026. It also shows Estimated conception: Thursday, January 15, 2026; End of first trimester: Wednesday, April 1, 2026.

Good to know

Only about 1 in 20 babies arrive on the due date. Your doctor may change the date after an ultrasound.
