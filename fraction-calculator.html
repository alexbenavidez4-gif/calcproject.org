How it works, examples and FAQ

Adding and subtracting need a common denominator. Find the least common multiple of the two bottoms, rewrite both fractions over it, then add or subtract the tops. 1/2 + 1/3 becomes 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Multiplying is straight across: tops times tops, bottoms times bottoms.

Dividing means multiplying by the reciprocal. Flip the second fraction, then multiply.

The answer is always reduced by dividing the top and bottom by their greatest common factor. Improper fractions also get shown as mixed numbers.

How do I enter a mixed number like 2 1/2?
Convert it first: 2 1/2 = (2 × 2 + 1)/2 = 5/2. Then enter 5 on top and 2 on the bottom.

How do I enter a whole number?
Put it over 1. For example, 3 is 3/1.

Why does the calculator say the bottom cannot be 0?
Dividing by zero is undefined, so a fraction with 0 on the bottom has no value.
