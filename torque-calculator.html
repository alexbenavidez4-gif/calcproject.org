The formula

Torque = force × lever arm × sin(angle).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Force: 50; Lever arm: 0.4; Angle between force and arm: 90), the result is Torque 20.00 N·m. It also shows Foot-pounds: 14.75 ft-lb.

Good to know

A longer wrench gives more torque for the same push.
