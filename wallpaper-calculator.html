How to use the wallpaper calculator

  1. Measure the room length, width, and wall height.
  2. Count the doors and windows.
  3. Enter the usable coverage per roll. A US single roll is roughly 25 sq ft usable after trimming.
  4. Round up, and buy every roll from the same dye lot.

The formula

Net wall area divided by usable square footage per roll after trimming.

Worked example

For example, a 12×10 ft room needs about 7 rolls at 25 usable sq ft per roll.

Good to know

  • The larger the pattern repeat, the more waste. For a repeat over 18 inches, lower the usable coverage by 15 to 20 percent.
  • Most wallpaper is sold in double rolls even when priced by the single roll. Check the label.
  • For one accent wall, multiply the wall width by its height and divide by the usable coverage per roll.

Common questions

How many square feet does a roll of wallpaper cover?

A standard US single roll holds about 27 to 30 square feet, with around 25 usable after trimming and matching. A double roll covers about twice that.

How do I account for pattern repeat?

Each strip has to start at the same point in the pattern, so part of every strip is cut away. Lower the usable coverage per roll, or add one roll for every five.

Should I subtract doors and windows?

Yes for standard doors and windows, but keep at least one spare roll. You still need full strips above and below most openings.
