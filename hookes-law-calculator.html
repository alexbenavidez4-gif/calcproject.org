The formula

F = k × x. Energy stored = ½ × k × x².

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Spring constant: 200; Stretch or compression: 0.05), the result is Force 10.00 N. It also shows Energy stored: 0.250 J.

Good to know

It holds as long as the spring is not stretched past its elastic limit.
