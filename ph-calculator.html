The formula

pH = −log10 of the hydrogen ion concentration. pOH = 14 − pH at 25 °C.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Hydrogen ion concentration: 0.0001), the result is pH 4.00. It also shows pOH: 10.00; Type: Acidic.

Good to know

Each pH step is a tenfold change in acidity.
