The formula

Velocity (ft/s) = 0.4085 × gallons per minute ÷ diameter (in)².

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Flow rate: 10; Inside diameter: 1), the result is Velocity 4.09 ft/s. It also shows Meters per second: 1.25 m/s.

Good to know

Home water lines are usually sized to stay under 5 to 8 feet per second.
