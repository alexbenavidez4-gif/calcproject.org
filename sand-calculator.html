How to use the sand calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Enter the depth in inches: 1 inch under pavers, 2 inches under a pool, 6 to 12 inches for a sandbox.
  3. Leave the weight at 1.35 tons per cubic yard for dry sand, or use your supplier's number.

The formula

Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Tons = cubic yards × 1.35. Bags = tons × 2,000 ÷ 50.

Worked example

A 12 × 10 ft paver bed with 1 inch of sand needs 0.37 cubic yards, which is 0.5 tons or 20 bags of 50 lb sand.

Good to know

  • Wet sand weighs more, up to about 1.6 tons per cubic yard.
  • Use coarse concrete sand under pavers, not fine play sand.
  • A 50 lb bag of sand is about half a cubic foot.

Common questions

How much does a cubic yard of sand weigh?

Dry sand weighs about 2,600 to 2,800 lb per cubic yard, or roughly 1.35 tons. Wet sand can reach 3,200 lb.

How many 50 lb bags of sand are in a cubic yard?

About 54 bags, since a cubic yard of dry sand weighs around 2,700 lb.

How much sand do I need under pavers?

One inch of bedding sand, screeded flat. For 100 square feet that is about 0.31 cubic yards, or 17 bags of 50 lb sand.
