The formula

For the matrix [a b; c d], the determinant is ad − bc. The inverse is 1 ÷ (ad − bc) times [d −b; −c a].

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Top left: 4; Top right: 7; Bottom left: 2; Bottom right: 6), the result is Determinant 10. It also shows Inverse, top row: 0.6, -0.7; Inverse, bottom row: -0.2, 0.4.

Good to know

A matrix with a determinant of 0 has no inverse.
