The formula

a mod b = a − b × floor(a ÷ b).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Dividend: 47; Divisor: 5), the result is Remainder 2. It also shows Whole quotient: 9; Check: 9 × 5 + 2 = 47.

Good to know

Clock time is modulo arithmetic: 15 hours after 10 o’clock is (10 + 15) mod 12 = 1 o’clock.
