How to use the roofing calculator

  1. Get the roof footprint in square feet from Google Maps or by multiplying the house length by its width, including overhangs.
  2. Enter the roof pitch as the rise per 12 inches of run.
  3. Set the waste allowance: 10 to 12 percent for a simple gable roof, 15 percent for hips and valleys.
  4. Leave the bundle coverage at 33.3 sq ft for standard three-bundle-per-square shingles.

The formula

Actual roof area = footprint × pitch multiplier (accounts for slope), plus waste, divided by bundle coverage. A 6/12 pitch multiplies footprint by about 1.118.

Worked example

For example, a 1,500 sq ft footprint at a 6/12 pitch works out to about 1,677 sq ft of actual roof — roughly 60 bundles at 33.3 sq ft each.

Good to know

  • A roofing square is 100 square feet. Most architectural shingles come three bundles to a square.
  • You also need starter strip, ridge caps, underlayment, drip edge, and nails.
  • Use the roof pitch calculator if you do not know your pitch.

Common questions

How many bundles of shingles do I need?

Divide the roof area in square feet by 100 to get squares, then multiply by 3 for standard shingles. A 1,700 square foot roof is 17 squares, or 51 bundles before waste.

How many square feet does a bundle of shingles cover?

About 33.3 square feet for most three-tab and architectural shingles. Some heavy designer shingles cover less, so check the wrapper.

How do I measure my roof without climbing on it?

Measure the footprint from the ground or a satellite map, then multiply by the pitch multiplier. This calculator does that step for you.
