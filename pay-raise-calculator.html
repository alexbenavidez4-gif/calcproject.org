How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Current annual pay and Raise.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

New pay = current pay × (1 + raise percent ÷ 100).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Current annual pay: 55000; Raise: 4), the result is New annual pay $57,200.00. It also shows Raise per year: $2,200.00; Raise per month: $183.33.

Good to know

A raise smaller than the inflation rate leaves you with less buying power than the year before.
