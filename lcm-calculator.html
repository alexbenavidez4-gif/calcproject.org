How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Numbers, separated by commas.

  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

LCM(a, b) = a × b ÷ GCF(a, b). For more numbers, work through the list two at a time.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Numbers, separated by commas: 4, 6, 15), the result is Least common multiple 60. It also shows Greatest common factor: 1.

Good to know

The least common multiple of the denominators is the least common denominator for adding fractions.
