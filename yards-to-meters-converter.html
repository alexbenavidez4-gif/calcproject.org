The formula

Meters = yards × 0.9144. One yard equals 0.9144 meters.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Yards: 10), the result is 10 yd = 9.144 m. It also shows 1 yd equals: 0.9144 m; 1 m equals: 1.0936 yd.
