How CalcProject Works

← All Calculators

CalcProject is a free set of calculators for home improvement projects — paint, flooring, tile, concrete, fencing, and more. No sign-up, no app, no account. Enter your measurements, get a number you can take straight to the store.

The idea is simple: measure once, calculate once, and buy the right amount the first time — instead of guessing, over-buying "just in case," or making a second trip because you came up short.

CalcProject is an independent side project, built and maintained by one person rather than a company. Every calculator shows its formula and a worked example right on the page — not just a final number, but how it got there.

These calculators give planning estimates, not exact contractor measurements. Always double-check against your own measurements, and check local building codes before buying materials for a project.
