How to use the brick calculator

  1. Measure the wall length and height in feet.
  2. Enter the face size of your brick: its length and height as laid.
  3. Enter the mortar joint. Three eighths of an inch is standard.

The formula

Bricks per square foot = 144 ÷ ((brick length + joint) × (brick height + joint)). Multiply by the wall area and add waste. Mortar is estimated at one 80 lb bag of premixed mortar for every 35 bricks.

Worked example

A modular brick (7 5/8 × 2 1/4 in) with a 3/8 inch joint works out to 6.86 bricks per square foot. A 20 × 4 ft wall is 80 sq ft, so with 10 percent waste buy 604 bricks and about 18 bags of mortar mix.

Good to know

  • This counts a single layer (one wythe). Double it for a wall two bricks thick.
  • Subtract door and window openings from the area for brick veneer on a house.
  • Order all the brick at once. Color varies between production runs.

Common questions

How many bricks do I need per square foot?

About 7 standard modular bricks per square foot with a 3/8 inch mortar joint. Larger queen or king size bricks need fewer, about 5 to 6.

How many bricks are in a pallet?

Usually around 500 standard modular bricks, though it varies by brick size and supplier.

How much mortar do I need for 1,000 bricks?

Roughly 28 to 30 bags of 80 lb premixed mortar, depending on joint thickness and waste.
