What This Is

CalcProject is a free set of calculators for home improvement projects — paint, flooring, tile, drywall, wallpaper, trim, mulch, concrete, gravel, fence, deck, and sod — plus a handful of guides on the questions that come up before you actually buy materials. No account, no sign-up, and every calculator works in both imperial and metric.

Why It Exists

Most people run into the same problem on a DIY project: guessing at how much material to buy, then either running short mid-project or overbuying and eating the cost of a return. CalcProject exists to take the guesswork out of that step — plug in real measurements, get a straight answer, and get back to the actual project.
