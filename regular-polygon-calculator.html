The formula

Area = n × s² ÷ (4 × tan(π ÷ n)). Each interior angle = (n − 2) × 180 ÷ n.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Number of sides: 8; Side length: 4), the result is Area 77.2548. It also shows Perimeter: 32; Apothem (center to side): 4.8284; Each interior angle: 135.00°.

Good to know

A regular polygon has all sides and all angles equal.
