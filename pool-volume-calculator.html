The formula

Gallons = surface area × average depth × 7.48. Rectangle area = length × width. Round area = 3.1416 × radius².

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Pool shape: Rectangle; Length or diameter: 30 ft; Width: 15 ft; Average depth: 5 ft), the result is Water 16,831 gal. It also shows Surface area: 450 sq ft; Volume: 2,250 cu ft.

Good to know

  • Average depth = (shallow end + deep end) ÷ 2. You need the volume to dose chlorine and other pool chemicals.
  • A round pool 24 ft across and 4 ft deep holds about 13,500 gallons.
  • For an oval or kidney pool, use the average length and width and multiply the result by 0.9.

Common questions

How do I calculate how many gallons my pool holds?

Multiply length by width by average depth in feet to get cubic feet, then multiply by 7.48. A 30 × 15 ft pool that averages 5 ft deep holds about 16,800 gallons.

How do I find the average depth?

Add the depth of the shallow end to the depth of the deep end and divide by 2.

Why do I need to know my pool volume?

Chlorine, shock, salt, and every other chemical are dosed per 10,000 gallons. The volume also sets the pump and filter size.
