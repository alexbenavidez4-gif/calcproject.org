How to use the pavers calculator

  1. Measure the patio or walkway length and width.
  2. Enter the paver size in inches from the product label.
  3. Set the base depth: 4 inches for patios and walks, 6 to 8 inches for driveways.

The formula

Pavers = patio area ÷ the area of one paver, plus waste. Gravel base = area × base depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27 cubic yards. Bedding sand uses the same math at 1 inch deep.

Worked example

A 12 × 10 ft patio is 120 sq ft. With 4 × 8 inch pavers and 10 percent waste, buy 594 pavers, 1.48 cubic yards of gravel for a 4 inch base, and 0.37 cubic yards of sand.

Good to know

  • Curved edges and diagonal patterns need 15 percent waste because every border paver is cut.
  • Compact the gravel base in 2 inch layers. A poor base is why pavers sink.
  • Plan for edge restraint and polymeric sand for the joints. About one 50 lb bag covers 50 to 75 sq ft.

Common questions

How many pavers do I need per square foot?

For 4 × 8 inch pavers, 4.5 per square foot. For 6 × 9 inch pavers, 2.67. For 12 × 12 inch pavers, 1.

How deep should the base be under pavers?

Four inches of compacted gravel plus 1 inch of sand for patios and walkways. Use 6 to 8 inches of gravel for a driveway or clay soil.

How much does a pallet of pavers cover?

Typically 100 to 120 square feet for standard 4 × 8 inch pavers, but it varies by product. The coverage is on the pallet tag.
