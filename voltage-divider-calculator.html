How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Input voltage, R1, top resistor and R2, bottom resistor.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Vout = Vin × R2 ÷ (R1 + R2).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Input voltage: 12; R1, top resistor: 1000; R2, bottom resistor: 2200), the result is Output voltage 8.250 V. It also shows Current through the divider: 3.750 mA.

Good to know

The output drops when you connect a load, so keep the load resistance much higher than R2.
