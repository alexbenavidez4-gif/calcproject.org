The formula

Weight = mass × gravitational acceleration (W = m × g).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Mass: 70; Gravity: 9.80665), the result is Weight 686.5 N. It also shows Pounds-force: 154.3 lbf; Weight on the Moon: 113.4 N; Weight on Mars: 259.7 N.

Good to know

Mass is the amount of matter and stays the same anywhere. Weight is a force and changes with gravity.
