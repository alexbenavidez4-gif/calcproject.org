How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Gross annual income.

  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Affordable rent = gross monthly income × 30 percent. Many landlords require income of at least three times the rent.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Gross annual income: 60000), the result is Rent at 30% of income $1,500.00. It also shows Gross monthly income: $5,000.00; At 25% of income: $1,250.00; Income most landlords want (3 times rent): $1,666.67.

Good to know

The 30 percent rule is a guideline. Heavy debt or high local costs can make a lower share safer.
