How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Negotiated price, Down payment and trade-in, Residual value, Money factor, Lease term and Sales tax.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Depreciation = (price − down payment − residual) ÷ months. Finance charge = (price − down payment + residual) × money factor. Payment = depreciation + finance charge, plus tax.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Negotiated price: 35000; Down payment and trade-in: 2000; Residual value: 21000; Money factor: 0.0025; Lease term: 36; Sales tax: 6.25), the result is Monthly payment $497.60. It also shows Depreciation part: $333.33; Finance part: $135.00; Equivalent interest rate: 6.00%; Payment before tax: $468.33.

Good to know

Multiply the money factor by 2,400 to see it as an interest rate. Acquisition and other fees are not included.
