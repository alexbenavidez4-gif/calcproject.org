How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Tank diameter and Tank length or height.
  2. Use the Imperial and Metric switch if you measure in meters and centimeters.
  3. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  4. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Gallons = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × length ÷ 231, with both in inches.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Tank diameter: 48 in; Tank length or height: 72 in), the result is Capacity 564 gal. It also shows Volume: 75.4 cu ft.

Good to know

There are 231 cubic inches in a US gallon. This is the full capacity of a tank with flat ends.
