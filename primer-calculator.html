How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Area to prime and Coverage.
  2. Use the Imperial and Metric switch if you measure in meters and centimeters.
  3. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  4. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Gallons = area ÷ coverage per gallon, rounded up to the next quart.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Area to prime: 600 sq ft; Coverage: 250 sq ft per gallon), the result is Buy 2.50 gal.

Good to know

Primer covers less than paint, usually 200 to 300 sq ft per gallon. Bare drywall and raw wood are at the low end.
