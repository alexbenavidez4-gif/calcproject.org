How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Loan amount, Upfront fees, Interest rate and Loan term.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

The payment is figured on the full loan amount at the stated rate. APR is the rate that gives the same payment on the amount you actually receive after fees.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Loan amount: 20000; Upfront fees: 600; Interest rate: 7; Loan term: 5), the result is APR 8.286%. It also shows Monthly payment: $396.02; Amount you actually receive: $19,400.00.

Good to know

APR lets you compare loans with different fees. A loan with a lower rate but high fees can have a higher APR.
