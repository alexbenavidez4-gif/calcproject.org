The formula

Sheets = area (length × width), plus waste, divided by the sheet size (32 sq ft for a 4x8), rounded up.

Good to know

  • For roof sheathing, use the real roof area, not the footprint. Multiply the footprint by the pitch multiplier first.
  • Leave a 1/8 inch gap between sheets so they can swell without buckling.
  • Common thicknesses: 7/16 inch OSB or 1/2 inch plywood for roofs and walls, and 3/4 inch tongue-and-groove for subfloors.

Common questions

How many square feet is a sheet of plywood?

A standard 4 × 8 ft sheet covers 32 square feet.

How many sheets of plywood do I need for 1,000 square feet?

A thousand square feet is 31.25 sheets. With 10 percent waste, buy 35 sheets of 4 × 8.

Is OSB the same size as plywood?

Yes. OSB and plywood sheathing both come in 4 × 8 ft sheets, so the count is the same.
