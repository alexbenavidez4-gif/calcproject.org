Common questions

How do I calculate the cost to run an appliance?

Multiply the watts by the hours used and divide by 1,000 to get kilowatt-hours. Multiply the kilowatt-hours by your rate per kWh. A 1,500 W heater running 8 hours uses 12 kWh, which costs $1.80 at $0.15 per kWh.

Where do I find the wattage of an appliance?

On the label or nameplate, usually on the back or bottom. If only amps are listed, multiply the amps by the voltage (120 V for most outlets) to get watts.

What is a kilowatt-hour?

A kilowatt-hour (kWh) is 1,000 watts used for one hour. It is the unit your electric company bills you for.
