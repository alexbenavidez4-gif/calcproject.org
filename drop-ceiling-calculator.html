The formula

Tiles = ceiling area ÷ tile area (4 sq ft for 2x2, 8 sq ft for 2x4), plus waste. Wall angle = room perimeter.

Good to know

  • Center the grid so the border tiles on opposite walls are the same width.
  • A suspended grid needs at least 3 to 4 inches of clearance below the joists to tilt the tiles in.
  • Main runners usually go every 4 ft, with cross tees every 2 ft between them.

Common questions

How many ceiling tiles do I need?

Divide the ceiling area by the tile area: 4 square feet for a 2 × 2 tile and 8 for a 2 × 4. A 16 × 12 ft room takes 48 tiles of 2 × 2, or 53 with waste.

Should I use 2x2 or 2x4 ceiling tiles?

Two by four tiles are cheaper and faster to install. Two by two tiles look more finished and sag less.

How much wall angle do I need?

Enough to go around the whole room, so the same as the room perimeter. It comes in 10 or 12 ft lengths.
