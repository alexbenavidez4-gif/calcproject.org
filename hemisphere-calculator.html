How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Radius.

  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Volume = (2 ÷ 3) × π × r³. Curved area = 2 × π × r². With the flat base, total area = 3 × π × r².

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Radius: 5), the result is Volume 261.7994. It also shows Curved surface area: 157.0796; Total surface area with the flat base: 235.6194.
