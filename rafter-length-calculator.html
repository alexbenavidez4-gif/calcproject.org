The formula

Rafter length = run × √(1 + (pitch ÷ 12)²). The overhang is multiplied by the same factor.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Run, wall to ridge: 12 ft; Roof pitch: 6; Overhang, measured level: 12 in), the result is Total rafter length 14.53 ft. It also shows Pitch multiplier: 1.118x; Rafter, wall to ridge: 13.42 ft; Overhang along the slope: 1.12 ft.

Good to know

  • Run is half the building width for a centered ridge. Buy the next stock length up, and subtract half the ridge board thickness when you cut.
  • Common pitch multipliers: 4/12 is 1.054, 6/12 is 1.118, 8/12 is 1.202, and 12/12 is 1.414.
  • This is the length along the top edge of the rafter. The birdsmouth cut at the wall does not change it.

Common questions

How do I calculate rafter length?

Multiply the horizontal run by the pitch multiplier, which is the square root of 1 + (pitch ÷ 12) squared. A 12 ft run at a 6/12 pitch gives a 13.42 ft rafter before the overhang.

What is the run of a rafter?

The level distance from the outside of the wall to the center of the ridge. On a simple gable roof it is half the width of the building.

What size lumber do I need for rafters?

That depends on span, spacing, roof load, and wood species, and it is set by span tables in your building code. This calculator gives length only.
