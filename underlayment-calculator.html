How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Room length, Room width, Coverage per roll and Waste allowance.
  2. Use the Imperial and Metric switch if you measure in meters and centimeters.
  3. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  4. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Rolls = room area, plus waste, divided by the square feet per roll.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Room length: 14 ft; Room width: 12 ft; Coverage per roll: 100 sq ft; Waste allowance: 5), the result is Buy 2 rolls. It also shows Room area: 168.0 sq ft.

Good to know

Many vinyl plank floors have a pad attached and should not get a second underlayment. Check the flooring instructions.
