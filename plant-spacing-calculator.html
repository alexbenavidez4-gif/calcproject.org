The formula

Square grid = (bed length ÷ spacing) × (bed width ÷ spacing), each rounded down. Staggered rows fit about 15 percent more: area ÷ (spacing² × 0.866).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Bed length: 12 ft; Bed width: 4 ft; Spacing between plants: 12 in), the result is Square grid 48 plants. It also shows Bed area: 48.0 sq ft; Triangular (staggered) rows: 55 plants.

Good to know

  • Use the mature width on the plant tag as the spacing.
  • Plants per square foot = 144 ÷ the spacing in inches squared. At 12 inches that is 1 per square foot. At 18 inches it is 0.44.
  • Stagger the rows for ground covers so the bed fills in evenly.

Common questions

How many plants do I need for my bed?

Divide the bed length and width by the spacing and multiply. A 12 × 4 ft bed at 12 inch spacing holds 48 plants, or 16 at 18 inch spacing.

How far apart should I space plants?

Use the mature width on the plant tag. Plants set at that distance will just touch when fully grown.

What is triangular spacing?

Each row is offset by half a space from the one before. It fits about 15 percent more plants in the same area and covers bare ground faster.
