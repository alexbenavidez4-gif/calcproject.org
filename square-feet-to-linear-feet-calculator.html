The formula

Linear feet = square feet × 12 ÷ board width (in).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Area to cover: 200 sq ft; Board width: 5.5 in), the result is Linear feet 436.4 ft.

Good to know

  • Use the actual face width of the board, not the nominal size. A 1x6 is 5.5 inches wide.
  • Square feet cannot be converted to linear feet without a width. The narrower the board, the more linear feet you need.
  • Add 10 percent for cuts before you order.

Common questions

How many linear feet are in 100 square feet?

It depends on the board width. With 5.5 inch boards, 100 square feet takes about 218 linear feet. With 3.25 inch flooring it takes about 369.

What is the difference between linear feet and square feet?

A linear foot measures length only. A square foot measures area, which is length times width.

Which width should I use for tongue-and-groove boards?

Use the exposed face after the boards lock together, not the full width including the tongue.
