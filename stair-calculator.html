How to use the stairs calculator

  1. Measure the total rise from the finished lower floor to the finished upper floor or deck surface.
  2. Set your target riser height. Seven to 7.5 inches is comfortable.
  3. Set the tread depth. Ten inches is the usual minimum.
  4. Buy stringer stock longer than the stringer length shown. A 2x12 is standard.

The formula

Number of risers = total rise ÷ target riser height, rounded, and never fewer than total rise ÷ 7.75. Riser height = total rise ÷ number of risers. Treads = risers − 1. Total run = treads × tread depth. Stringer length = square root of (rise² + run²).

Worked example

A 108 inch total rise with a 7.5 inch target gives 14 risers at 7.71 inches each and 13 treads. With 10 inch treads the total run is 10.83 ft and the stringer is about 14.08 ft.

Good to know

  • Every riser in a flight must be the same height within 3/8 inch, or people trip.
  • Measure the rise where the stairs will actually land. Ground and floors are rarely level.
  • A comfortable stair has a riser plus tread total of 17 to 18 inches.

Common questions

What is the maximum riser height for stairs?

The International Residential Code sets a maximum riser height of 7 3/4 inches and a minimum tread depth of 10 inches for homes. Local codes can differ.

How many steps do I need for a 9 foot ceiling?

A 9 ft ceiling usually means a floor-to-floor rise of about 118 to 121 inches once the floor framing is included, which takes 16 risers. An 8 ft ceiling is about 106 to 109 inches, which takes 14 or 15.

How long of a board do I need for stair stringers?

Take the stringer length from the calculator and round up to the next stock length. A 14 ft stringer needs a 16 ft 2x12.
