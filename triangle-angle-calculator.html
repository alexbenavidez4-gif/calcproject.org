How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Side a, Side b and Side c.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Law of cosines: cos(A) = (b² + c² − a²) ÷ (2bc), and the same pattern for the other angles.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Side a: 5; Side b: 6; Side c: 7), the result is Largest angle 78.46°. It also shows Angle A (opposite side a): 44.42°; Angle B (opposite side b): 57.12°; Angle C (opposite side c): 78.46°.

Good to know

The three angles of any triangle add up to 180 degrees.
