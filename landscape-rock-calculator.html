The formula

Cubic yards = length × width × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Tons = cubic yards × weight per yard.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Area length: 20 ft; Area width: 8 ft; Depth: 2 in; Weight: 1.3 tons per cubic yard), the result is Buy 1.28 tons. It also shows Volume: 0.99 cu yd.

Good to know

  • Use 2 inches deep for stone under 1 inch and 3 to 4 inches for larger river rock. Lay landscape fabric underneath.
  • One ton covers about 125 square feet at 2 inches deep.
  • One cubic yard equals 54 bags of 0.5 cubic foot rock, so bulk delivery is far cheaper for large areas.

Common questions

How much landscape rock do I need?

Multiply the area in square feet by the depth in feet and divide by 27 for cubic yards. A 20 × 8 ft bed at 2 inches takes about 1 cubic yard, or 1.3 tons.

How deep should landscape rock be?

Two inches for rock under 1 inch in size, and 3 to 4 inches for larger river rock so the fabric does not show.

Should I put fabric under rock?

Yes. Landscape fabric keeps the rock from sinking into the soil and slows weeds. Use fabric, not plastic, so water can drain.
