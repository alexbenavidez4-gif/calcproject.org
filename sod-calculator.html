How to use the sod calculator

  1. Measure the lawn length and width in feet. Split odd shapes into rectangles and add them.
  2. Set the waste allowance: 5 percent for a simple rectangle, 10 percent for curves.
  3. Ask your sod farm how many square feet are on a pallet. It ranges from 400 to 500.

The formula

Lawn area plus waste, divided by square footage per pallet.

Worked example

For example, a 40×30 ft lawn needs about 3 pallets.

Good to know

  • Lay sod the day it arrives. It starts to heat up and yellow on the pallet within about 24 hours in warm weather.
  • Subtract the house, driveway, and beds from your lot measurements.
  • Water new sod daily for the first two weeks.

Common questions

How many square feet is a pallet of sod?

Usually 400 to 500 square feet, with 450 the most common. It depends on the farm and the grass type.

How much sod do I need for 1,000 square feet?

With 5 percent waste you need 1,050 square feet, which is 3 pallets at 450 square feet each.

How much does a pallet of sod weigh?

Between 1,500 and 3,000 lb depending on moisture. Most half-ton pickups cannot safely carry a full pallet.
