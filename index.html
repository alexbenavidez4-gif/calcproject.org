How these calculators work

Each calculator takes the measurements you can get with a tape measure, such as length, width, height, or depth, and turns them into what the store sells: gallons, boxes, bags, sheets, boards, bundles, pallets, cubic yards, or tons. The formula and a worked example are shown on every page, so you can check the math yourself.

Every home project calculator has an Imperial and Metric switch and a waste allowance for cuts, breakage, and mistakes. When you are done, tap Save or print results. Every calculator you use in one visit goes onto one clean sheet that you can print or save as a PDF to pull up at the store.

Common questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator on CalcProject is free to use, with no account, no sign-up, and no app to install.

How accurate are the estimates?

The math is exact for the numbers you enter. Real projects vary with product coverage, layout, and waste, so each calculator includes a waste allowance and you should always round up.

Can I use metric units?

Yes. Every home project calculator has an Imperial and Metric switch. It converts your inputs and shows results in meters, square meters, liters, and kilograms.

Can I save or print my results?

Yes. Tap Save or print results on any calculator. Every home project calculator you use in one visit goes onto a single sheet that you can print or save as a PDF on your phone.

Estimates are a starting point for shopping, not a substitute for a contractor's exact measurement. Always round up, check the coverage printed on your product, and keep your receipt for returns.
