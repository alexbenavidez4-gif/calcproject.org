Indoor Projects

Paint Calculator

Figure out how many gallons a room actually needs, including doors, windows, and a second coat.

Job: Interior Paint
How it works: wall area = (2 × length + 2 × width) × height, minus door and window area, × number of coats, ÷ coverage. Example: a 12×10 ft room with 8 ft walls, one door, two windows, and two coats needs roughly 2 gallons at 350 sq ft/gallon coverage.

Flooring Calculator

Convert room size into square footage and boxes needed, with waste built in for cuts.

Job: Flooring
How it works: area = length × width, plus your waste percentage, divided by how many square feet each box covers, rounded up to a full box. Example: a 14×11 ft room (154 sq ft) with 10% waste needs about 170 sq ft of material — 9 boxes if each box covers 20 sq ft.

Tile Calculator

Figure out how many tiles a floor or wall needs, based on your exact tile size.

Job: Tile
How it works: area = length × width, plus waste, divided by the square footage of one tile. Example: a 10×8 ft floor with 12×12 in tiles and 10% waste needs about 88 tiles.

Drywall Calculator

Convert wall dimensions into the number of sheets you need to buy.

Job: Drywall
How it works: wall area minus door/window area, divided by sheet size. Example: a 12×10 ft room with 8 ft walls needs about 8 sheets of standard 4x8 drywall.

Wallpaper Calculator

Estimate rolls needed, with room left over for pattern matching and trimming.

Job: Wallpaper
How it works: net wall area divided by usable square footage per roll after trimming. Example: a 12×10 ft room needs about 7 rolls at 25 usable sq ft per roll.

Baseboard & Trim Calculator

Turn room perimeter into the number of boards to buy.

Job: Trim
How it works: perimeter minus doorway widths, plus waste, divided by board length. Example: a 12×10 ft room with one 3 ft doorway needs about 6 boards at 8 ft each.
Outdoor

Outdoor Projects

Mulch & Soil Calculator

Find the volume or bags you need for a bed or garden area, at any depth.

Job: Mulch & Soil
How it works: volume = length × width × depth, then converted to bags or bulk units. Example: a 20×6 ft bed at 3 inches deep needs about 1.1 cubic yards — roughly 15 bags at 2 cubic ft each.

Concrete Calculator

Find the volume and bags needed for a slab, footing, or pad.

Job: Concrete
How it works: volume = length × width × thickness, then ÷ bag size for bag count. Example: a 10×10 ft slab at 4 inches needs about 1.23 cubic yards — roughly 56 bags at 80lb each.

Gravel Calculator

Estimate volume and total weight for a driveway, path, or drainage bed.

Job: Gravel
How it works: volume × density gives total weight. Example: a 20×10 ft path at 4 inches deep needs about 3.5 tons of gravel.

Fence Calculator

Turn fence line length into posts and panels needed.

Job: Fence
How it works: posts = (fence length ÷ spacing) + 1, panels = fence length ÷ panel width. Example: 120 linear ft with 8 ft spacing needs 16 posts and 15 panels.

Deck Calculator

Estimate decking boards needed, accounting for board width and gap spacing.

Job: Deck
How it works: deck area ÷ (board width + gap) gives total board length, plus waste, divided by board length. Example: a 16×12 ft deck needs about 35 boards at 12 ft each.

Sod Calculator

Convert lawn area into pallets of sod needed.

Job: Sod
How it works: lawn area plus waste, divided by square footage per pallet. Example: a 40×30 ft lawn needs about 3 pallets.

Estimates are a starting point for shopping, not a substitute for a contractor's exact measurement — always round up and keep your receipt for returns.
