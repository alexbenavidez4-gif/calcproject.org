How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Hours worked each day, separated by commas, Hourly wage and Overtime after this many hours.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Gross pay = regular hours × wage + overtime hours × wage × 1.5.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Hours worked each day, separated by commas: 8, 8.5, 7.75, 9, 8; Hourly wage: 22; Overtime after this many hours: 40), the result is Gross pay $921.25. It also shows Regular hours: 40.00 hours; Overtime hours: 1.25 hours; Overtime pay at time and a half: $41.25; Total hours: 41.25 hours.

Good to know

Enter hours as decimals. 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75.
