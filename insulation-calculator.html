How to use the insulation calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the attic floor, wall, or crawl space.
  2. Find the coverage in square feet on the package for the R-value and width you are buying.
  3. For walls, enter the wall length as the length and the wall height as the width.

The formula

Packages = area (length × width) plus waste, divided by the square feet each package covers, rounded up.

Worked example

A 40 × 30 ft attic is 1,200 sq ft. With 5 percent waste that is 1,260 sq ft, or 22 packages at 58.67 sq ft each.

Source: US Department of Energy: Insulation and recommended R-values

Good to know

  • Match batt width to your framing: 15 inch batts for studs 16 inches on center, 23 inch for 24 inches on center.
  • Do not compress batts to fit. Squeezed insulation loses R-value.
  • Blown-in insulation is figured from the coverage chart on the bag, which lists bags per 1,000 sq ft for each R-value.

Common questions

What R-value do I need?

It depends on your climate zone and where the insulation goes. The US Department of Energy publishes recommended R-values by zone for attics, walls, and floors.

How many square feet does a bag of insulation cover?

It varies with R-value and width. Common packages cover roughly 40 to 90 square feet. The exact figure is printed on the bag.

Should I subtract the framing from the area?

No. Batt coverage figures already assume framing at standard spacing, so use the full area.
