How to use this converter

The formula

°C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9. °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32. K = °C + 273.15.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Temperature: 72; From: Fahrenheit; To: Celsius), the result is 72 °F = 22.22 °C. It also shows Fahrenheit: 72.00 °F; Celsius: 22.22 °C; Kelvin: 295.37 K.

Good to know

Water freezes at 32 °F (0 °C) and boils at 212 °F (100 °C) at sea level.
