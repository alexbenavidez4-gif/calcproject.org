The formula

Liner length = pond length + 2 × depth + 2 × overlap. Liner width = pond width + 2 × depth + 2 × overlap.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Pond length: 10 ft; Pond width: 6 ft; Maximum depth: 2 ft; Overlap on each side: 1 ft), the result is Liner area 192 sq ft. It also shows Liner length: 16.0 ft; Liner width: 12.0 ft.

Good to know

  • Measure the longest and widest points of an irregular pond. Buy underlayment the same size.
  • Lay underlayment or old carpet under the liner to protect it from rocks and roots.
  • Leave the extra liner around the edge until the pond is full, then trim it.

Common questions

What size pond liner do I need?

Liner length = pond length + twice the depth + twice the overlap. For a 10 × 6 ft pond that is 2 ft deep with a 1 ft overlap, you need a 16 × 12 ft liner.

How much overlap should a pond liner have?

At least 1 ft on every side, so the edge can be buried or held under stone.

How many gallons does my pond hold?

Multiply length by width by average depth in feet, then by 7.48. Irregular ponds hold about 80 to 85 percent of that figure.
