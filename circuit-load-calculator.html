How it works: total watts ÷ volts = amps on the circuit. Things that run for 3 hours or more (heaters, chargers) should stay under 80% of the breaker, so a 20 amp circuit tops out at 16 amps of steady load. Example: a 1,500 W heater plus a 100 W TV and a 60 W lamp is 1,660 W, or 13.8 amps, which fits a 20 amp circuit but is over the steady-use limit on a 15 amp circuit.
⚠️ Estimates only. If a breaker trips often, have an electrician check the circuit. Never swap in a bigger breaker without checking the wire size.

Common questions

How many outlets can be on a 20 amp circuit?

There is no fixed number for homes in the National Electrical Code. What matters is the total load. Keep steady loads under 16 amps (1,920 watts at 120 V) on a 20 amp circuit.

What is the 80 percent rule for breakers?

A load that runs for 3 hours or more should not exceed 80 percent of the breaker rating: 12 amps on a 15 amp breaker and 16 amps on a 20 amp breaker.

Why does my breaker keep tripping?

Usually because too much is running on one circuit at once. A breaker that trips with a light load can point to a wiring fault and should be checked by an electrician.
