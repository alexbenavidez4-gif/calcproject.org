How to use the board feet calculator

  1. Enter the thickness and width in inches and the length in feet.
  2. Enter how many boards of that size you need.
  3. Add the price per board foot from the lumber yard to see the cost.

The formula

Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12. One board foot is a piece 1 inch thick, 12 inches wide, and 1 foot long.

Worked example

Ten boards that are 1 in thick, 6 in wide, and 8 ft long are 4 board feet each, 40 board feet in total. At $4.50 per board foot that is $180.

Good to know

  • Hardwood thickness is quoted in quarters: 4/4 is 1 inch, 5/4 is 1.25 inches, 8/4 is 2 inches.
  • Lumber yards figure board feet from the rough size, before planing, so enter the nominal thickness.
  • Buy 15 to 20 percent extra hardwood to work around knots, checks, and grain matching.

Common questions

What is a board foot?

A board foot is a volume of wood equal to 144 cubic inches: 1 inch thick by 12 inches wide by 12 inches long.

How many board feet are in a 2x4x8?

Using the nominal size, 2 × 4 × 8 ÷ 12 = 5.33 board feet.

Is a board foot the same as a linear foot?

No. A linear foot only measures length. A board foot measures volume, so a wider or thicker board has more board feet per linear foot.
