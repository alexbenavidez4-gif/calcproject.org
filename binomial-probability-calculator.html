How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Number of trials, Number of successes and Chance of success each trial.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

P(exactly k) = nCk × pk × (1 − p)n − k.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Number of trials: 10; Number of successes: 3; Chance of success each trial: 50), the result is Exactly 3 11.719%. It also shows At most 3: 17.188%; At least 3: 94.531%; Expected successes: 5.

Good to know

It applies when each trial has two outcomes, the same chance every time, and the trials do not affect each other.
