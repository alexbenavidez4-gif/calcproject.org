How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Savings goal, Already saved, APY and Years to reach it.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Monthly deposit = (goal − start × (1 + r)months) × r ÷ ((1 + r)months − 1), where r is the monthly rate.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Savings goal: 20000; Already saved: 2000; APY: 4; Years to reach it: 3), the result is Save per month $465.38. It also shows Still needed after growth: $17,750.27; Per week: $107.40.

Good to know

If the result is $0, your current savings will reach the goal on interest alone.
