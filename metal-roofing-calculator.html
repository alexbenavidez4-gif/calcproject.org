How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Eave length, Panel length, eave to ridge, Panel coverage width and Roof sides.

The formula

Panels per side = eave length (in) ÷ panel coverage width, rounded up. Total = panels per side × number of sides.

Worked example

Good to know

  • Panels are ordered cut to length. Measure from the eave to the ridge along the slope and add the overhang.
  • Plan on about 80 screws for every 100 square feet of exposed-fastener panels.
  • Most exposed-fastener panels need a roof pitch of at least 3/12.

Common questions

How many metal roofing panels do I need?

Divide the eave length in inches by the panel coverage width. A 40 ft eave with 36 inch panels takes 14 panels on each side.

How wide is a metal roofing panel?

Most ribbed panels cover 36 inches once the edges overlap. Standing seam panels usually cover 12 to 18 inches.

How long should the panels be?

From the ridge to the eave along the slope, plus 1 to 2 inches of overhang. Panels are cut to your length at the supplier.
