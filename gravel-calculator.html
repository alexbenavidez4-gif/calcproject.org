How to use the gravel calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Enter the depth in inches: 2 inches for a path, 4 inches for a driveway layer or patio base.
  3. Leave the weight at 1.4 tons per cubic yard unless your supplier gives a different figure.

The formula

Volume × density gives total weight.

Worked example

For example, a 20×10 ft path at 4 inches deep needs about 3.5 tons of gravel.

Good to know

  • Crushed stone packs to about 1.4 tons per cubic yard. Pea gravel and river rock run closer to 1.3 to 1.5.
  • A new driveway is built in layers: larger stone on the bottom, finer stone on top. Run the calculator once per layer.
  • Most suppliers have a delivery minimum. Ask before you order a small load.

Common questions

How much does a cubic yard of gravel weigh?

About 1.4 tons, or 2,800 lb, for common crushed stone. The exact weight depends on the stone type and how wet it is.

How many tons of gravel do I need for a driveway?

A 20 × 10 ft section 4 inches deep is 2.47 cubic yards, or about 3.5 tons. Multiply up for your driveway length.

How much area does a ton of gravel cover?

At 2 inches deep, one ton covers roughly 115 square feet. At 4 inches it covers about 58 square feet.
