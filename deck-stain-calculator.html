How to use the deck stain calculator

  1. Measure the deck length and width in feet.
  2. Set the extra for railings and stairs. Twenty percent suits a basic railing; use 40 percent or more for balusters on every side.
  3. Enter the coats and the coverage printed on the can.

The formula

Gallons = deck area (length × width), plus a percentage for railings and stairs, × number of coats, ÷ coverage per gallon, rounded up.

Worked example

A 16 × 12 ft deck is 192 sq ft. Adding 20 percent for railings and two coats gives 460.8 sq ft to cover. At 250 sq ft per gallon, buy 2 gallons.

Good to know

  • Rough or weathered wood drinks stain. Use 150 to 200 sq ft per gallon for old decks and for the first coat on new rough-sawn wood.
  • Many semi-transparent stains call for one coat only. Read the label before applying a second.
  • Stain when the wood is dry and no rain is due for 24 to 48 hours.

Common questions

How much stain do I need for a 12x12 deck?

A 12 × 12 ft deck is 144 square feet. One coat at 250 square feet per gallon takes about 1 gallon including a basic railing; two coats take 2 gallons.

How many square feet does a gallon of deck stain cover?

Usually 150 to 300 square feet. Smooth new wood is at the high end and rough or weathered wood at the low end.

Do I need two coats of deck stain?

Solid stains usually take two coats. Many transparent and semi-transparent stains are designed for a single coat.
