Convert between watts and amps for any device, like "how many amps is a 1,500 W heater?"

Job: Electrical
How it works: watts = volts × amps, so amps = watts ÷ volts. Example: a 1,500 W heater on a 120 V outlet draws 12.5 amps. That is fine on a 15 amp circuit by itself, but not with much else plugged in.
⚠️ Estimates only. Motors can draw more when starting than their label shows.

Common questions

How do I convert watts to amps?

Divide the watts by the volts. A 1,500 W heater on a 120 V outlet draws 1,500 ÷ 120 = 12.5 amps.

How many watts can a 15 amp circuit handle?

A 15 amp, 120 V circuit carries 1,800 watts at most, and 1,440 watts for loads that run 3 hours or more (80 percent of the breaker rating).

How many amps does a 1,500 watt heater use?

About 12.5 amps at 120 volts. That nearly fills a 15 amp circuit, so run it on a circuit with little else plugged in.
