How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Length, Width and Compacted thickness.
  2. Use the Imperial and Metric switch if you measure in meters and centimeters.
  3. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  4. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Tons = length × width × thickness (in) ÷ 12 × 145 lb per cubic foot ÷ 2,000.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Length: 40 ft; Width: 12 ft; Compacted thickness: 3 in), the result is Order 8.7 tons. It also shows Area: 480 sq ft; Volume: 4.44 cu yd.

Good to know

Hot mix asphalt weighs about 145 lb per cubic foot once compacted. Residential driveways are usually 2 to 3 inches thick over a gravel base.
