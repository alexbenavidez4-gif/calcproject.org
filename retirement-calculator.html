How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Current age, Retirement age, Saved so far, Monthly contribution and Rate of return.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Balance = saved × (1 + r)months + monthly contribution × ((1 + r)months − 1) ÷ r, where r is the monthly rate.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Current age: 35; Retirement age: 65; Saved so far: 50000; Monthly contribution: 600; Rate of return: 6), the result is Balance at retirement $903,837.79. It also shows Years to retirement: 30 years; Total contributed: $266,000.00; Income at a 4% yearly withdrawal: $36,153.51.

Good to know

Results are in future dollars and ignore taxes and fees. The 4 percent rule is a common starting point for retirement income, not a guarantee.
