The formula

Gravel = trench length × (width × depth − pipe area) ÷ 144, in cubic feet. Divide by 27 for cubic yards.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Trench length: 50 ft; Trench width: 12 in; Trench depth: 18 in; Pipe diameter: 4 in), the result is Order 2.62 cu yd. It also shows Gravel volume: 70.6 cu ft; In tons: 3.7 tons.

Good to know

  • Use clean washed stone, 3/4 to 1.5 inch, and wrap the trench in filter fabric. Slope the pipe at least 1 inch per 8 feet.
  • The pipe holes face down. Water rises into the pipe from below.
  • Send the outlet to daylight, a dry well, or a storm drain, never toward a neighbor or the foundation.

Common questions

How much gravel do I need for a French drain?

Multiply the trench length by its width and depth and subtract the pipe. A 50 ft trench that is 12 inches wide and 18 inches deep takes about 2.6 cubic yards.

How deep should a French drain be?

Eighteen to 24 inches for yard drainage. Drains that protect a basement go down to the footing.

What kind of gravel is best for a French drain?

Clean, washed stone from 3/4 to 1.5 inches with no fine material, which would clog the pipe.
