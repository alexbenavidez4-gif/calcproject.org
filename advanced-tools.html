Electrical Calculator Disclaimer

The electrical calculators on CalcProject.org provide general estimates for planning and educational purposes only. Results are based on common standards and simplified formulas and may not account for every factor in your situation, such as wire type, temperature, conduit fill, or load type.

Electrical code requirements vary by city, county, and state, and local codes may differ from national standards like the National Electrical Code (NEC). Always check with your local building department and consult a licensed electrician before doing any electrical work.

Working with electricity can cause serious injury, death, or fire. CalcProject.org is not responsible for any damage, injury, or loss resulting from the use of these calculators.
