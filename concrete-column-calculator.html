The formula

Volume = 3.1416 × radius² × height × number of columns. Bags are 80 lb at 0.60 cu ft each.

Good to know

  • A 12 inch tube form 4 ft tall takes about 3.14 cubic feet, a little over 5 bags of 80 lb mix.
  • An 8 inch tube 4 ft tall takes 3 bags of 80 lb mix. A 10 inch tube takes 4, and a 12 inch tube takes 6.
  • Brace tube forms plumb before you pour, and tap the sides to work out air pockets.

Common questions

How many bags of concrete do I need for a 12 inch tube form?

A 12 inch tube holds 0.785 cubic feet per foot of height. A 4 ft tall tube takes 3.14 cubic feet, which is 6 bags of 80 lb mix.

What size tube form do I need for a deck post?

It depends on the load and soil. Eight to 12 inch piers are common for decks, and your building department sets the minimum.

How deep should a concrete pier go?

Below the frost line for your area, and at least 12 inches into undisturbed soil. Frost depth runs from a few inches in the South to 4 ft or more in the North.
