How it works, examples and FAQ

Adding time: turn each time into seconds, add, then turn the total back into days, hours, minutes and seconds. 5 hours 45 minutes plus 3 hours 30 minutes 15 seconds is 20,700 + 12,615 = 33,315 seconds, which is 9 hours 15 minutes 15 seconds.

Subtracting time: do the same with subtraction. If the second time is longer, the answer is negative.

Time uses 60, not 100: 60 seconds in a minute, 60 minutes in an hour, and 24 hours in a day. That is why 1 hour 45 minutes plus 30 minutes is 2 hours 15 minutes, not 1 hour 75 minutes.

Leave any box empty to use 0. The answer is shown in days, hours, minutes and seconds, as hours:minutes:seconds, and as total hours, minutes and seconds.

How do I add hours and minutes?
Add the hours together, add the minutes together, and if the minutes reach 60, carry 1 hour. For example, 2 h 45 min + 1 h 30 min = 3 h 75 min = 4 h 15 min.

How do I subtract time?
Pick Subtract, enter the longer time first and the shorter time second. The calculator converts both to seconds, subtracts, and converts the answer back.

How many hours is 150 minutes?
150 minutes is 2 hours 30 minutes, or 2.5 hours. Enter 150 in the minutes box with Add and a second time of 0 to see it.

How many seconds are in a day?
There are 86,400 seconds in a day: 24 hours × 60 minutes × 60 seconds.

Can I use decimals like 1.5 hours?
Use whole numbers in each box. For 1.5 hours, enter 1 hour and 30 minutes.
