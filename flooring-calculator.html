How to use the flooring calculator

  1. Measure the room length and width at the longest points.
  2. Set the waste allowance: 10 percent for a straight layout, 15 percent for diagonal or herringbone.
  3. Enter the square feet per box from the product label.
  4. Round up to full boxes and keep one unopened for future repairs.

The formula

Area = length × width, plus your waste percentage, divided by how many square feet each box covers, rounded up to a full box.

Worked example

For example, a 14×11 ft room (154 sq ft) with 10% waste needs about 170 sq ft of material — 9 boxes if each box covers 20 sq ft.

Good to know

  • Include closets and the area under appliances you plan to floor under.
  • Stores usually take back unopened boxes, so rounding up is cheap insurance.
  • Buy all boxes from the same lot number so the color matches.

Common questions

How much extra flooring should I buy?

Buy 10 percent extra for a simple rectangular room and 15 percent for diagonal layouts, many angles, or a first-time install.

How many boxes of flooring do I need for 200 square feet?

With 10 percent waste you need 220 square feet. At 20 square feet per box that is 11 boxes.

How do I calculate flooring for more than one room?

Add the square footage of each room first, then apply the waste allowance once to the total.
