How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Amount today, Inflation rate and Years.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Future cost = amount × (1 + inflation rate)years. Purchasing power = amount ÷ the same factor.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Amount today: 1000; Inflation rate: 3; Years: 10), the result is Future cost $1,343.92. It also shows What $1 today will buy then: $0.74; Purchasing power of the amount then: $744.09.

Good to know

This uses a rate you choose. For past years, use published Consumer Price Index figures.
