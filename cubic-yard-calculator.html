How to use the cubic yards calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Decide how deep the material needs to be, in inches.
  3. Order the cubic yards shown, rounded up. Most suppliers sell in half-yard or full-yard steps.

The formula

Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Dividing the depth by 12 turns inches into feet, and dividing by 27 turns cubic feet into cubic yards.

Worked example

A 10 × 10 ft area filled 4 inches deep is 100 × 4 ÷ 12 = 33.3 cubic feet, or 1.23 cubic yards.

Good to know

  • One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, a cube 3 feet on each side.
  • A full-size pickup bed holds roughly 2 cubic yards of mulch by volume, but only about 1 yard of soil or gravel by weight.
  • Loose material settles. Add 5 to 10 percent for soil and mulch.

Common questions

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

There are 27 cubic feet in one cubic yard, because a yard is 3 feet and 3 × 3 × 3 = 27.

How much area does one cubic yard cover?

At 3 inches deep, one cubic yard covers about 108 square feet. At 4 inches it covers 81 square feet, and at 2 inches it covers 162 square feet.

How do I work out cubic yards for a circle?

Find the area first: 3.14 × radius × radius, in feet. Multiply by the depth in feet, then divide by 27.
