The formula

Cubic yards = length × width × thickness (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27. Cost = cubic yards × price per yard.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Driveway length: 40 ft; Driveway width: 12 ft; Thickness: 5 in; Price per cubic yard: 160), the result is Order 7.41 cu yd. It also shows Area: 480 sq ft; Concrete cost: $1,185.19.

Good to know

  • Order 5 to 10 percent extra. The price is entered per cubic yard in both unit modes and does not include forms, base, rebar, or labor.
  • Cut control joints about every 10 ft so the slab cracks along straight lines.
  • Keep vehicles off new concrete for at least 7 days.

Common questions

How thick should a concrete driveway be?

Four inches is the minimum for cars. Use 5 to 6 inches where trucks, RVs, or trailers will park.

How many yards of concrete do I need for a 20x20 driveway?

At 4 inches thick, a 20 × 20 ft slab takes 4.94 cubic yards. Order 5.5 to allow for uneven ground.

What does a concrete driveway cost?

The concrete itself is the smaller part. Forms, gravel base, reinforcement, finishing labor, and removing an old driveway usually cost more than the material.
