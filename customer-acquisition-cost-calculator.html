How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Sales and marketing spend and New customers.

The formula

CAC = sales and marketing spend ÷ new customers won.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Sales and marketing spend: 15000; New customers: 120), the result is Cost per customer $125.00.

Good to know

Compare CAC with customer lifetime value. A lifetime value of three times CAC is a common target.
