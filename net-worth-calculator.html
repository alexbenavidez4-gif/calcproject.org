How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Cash and savings, Investments and retirement, Home value, Vehicles and other assets, Mortgage balance and Other debts.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Net worth = total assets − total debts.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Cash and savings: 15000; Investments and retirement: 60000; Home value: 300000; Vehicles and other assets: 20000; Mortgage balance: 220000; Other debts: 18000), the result is Net worth $157,000.00. It also shows Total assets: $395,000.00; Total debts: $238,000.00.

Good to know

Track it once or twice a year. The direction matters more than the number.
