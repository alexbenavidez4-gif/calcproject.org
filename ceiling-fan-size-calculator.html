How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Room length and Room width.
  2. Use the Imperial and Metric switch if you measure in meters and centimeters.
  3. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  4. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Up to 75 sq ft: 29 to 36 inch fan. 76 to 144 sq ft: 36 to 44 inch. 145 to 225 sq ft: 44 to 50 inch. 226 to 400 sq ft: 50 to 54 inch. Larger rooms: 60 inch or two fans.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Room length: 14 ft; Room width: 12 ft), the result is Fan size 44 to 50 in. It also shows Room area: 168 sq ft.

Good to know

Hang the blades 8 to 9 feet above the floor and at least 18 inches from any wall.
