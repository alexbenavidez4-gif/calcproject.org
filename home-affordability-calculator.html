How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Gross annual income, Monthly debt payments, Down payment, Interest rate, Loan term and Taxes and insurance per month.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

The housing payment is capped at 28 percent of gross monthly income, and housing plus other debts at 36 percent. Taxes and insurance are subtracted, and the rest is turned into a loan amount at your rate and term.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Gross annual income: 90000; Monthly debt payments: 500; Down payment: 40000; Interest rate: 6.5; Loan term: 30; Taxes and insurance per month: 500), the result is Home price you can afford $293,137.31. It also shows Maximum housing payment: $2,100.00; Loan amount: $253,137.31.

Good to know

This is the 28/36 guideline many lenders start from. Actual approval depends on credit, loan type, and local costs.
