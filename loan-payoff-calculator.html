How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Loan balance, Interest rate and Monthly payment.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Months = −ln(1 − balance × r ÷ payment) ÷ ln(1 + r), where r is the monthly rate.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Loan balance: 15000; Interest rate: 8; Monthly payment: 400), the result is Months to pay off 44 months. It also shows Years: 3.6 years; Total interest: $2,318.40.

Good to know

The payment has to be larger than one month of interest or the balance never goes down.
