How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Start date and End date.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Count each day from the start date up to, but not including, the end date, and leave out Saturdays and Sundays.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Start date: 2026-01-01; End date: 2026-01-31), the result is Business days 22 days. It also shows Calendar days: 30 days; Weekend days: 8 days.

Good to know

Holidays are not removed. Subtract any that fall on a weekday.
