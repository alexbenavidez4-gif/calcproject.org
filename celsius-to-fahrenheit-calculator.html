How it works, examples and FAQ

Baking? See the oven temperature chart for Celsius, Fahrenheit and gas marks.

Celsius to Fahrenheit: multiply by 9/5, then add 32. For 25°C: 25 × 9 ÷ 5 = 45, and 45 + 32 = 77, so 25°C is 77°F.

Fahrenheit to Celsius: subtract 32, then multiply by 5/9. For 77°F: 77 − 32 = 45, and 45 × 5 ÷ 9 = 25, so 77°F is 25°C.

Quick check: a handy shortcut is to double the Celsius number and add 30 for a close estimate. The calculator gives the exact answer.

Common temperatures:

  • 0°C = 32°F (freezing point of water)
  • 20°C = 68°F (a comfortable room)
  • 25°C = 77°F (a warm day)
  • 37°C = 98.6°F (normal body temperature)
  • 100°C = 212°F (boiling point of water)
  • -40°C = -40°F (where both scales match)

How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 (or 1.8) and add 32. For example, 100°C × 1.8 + 32 = 212°F.

How do you convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?
Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature, then multiply by 5/9. For example, (212 − 32) × 5/9 = 100°C.

Is 30 degrees Celsius hot?
30°C is 86°F, which is a hot summer day.

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?
At -40 degrees. -40°C and -40°F are the same temperature.

What is absolute zero?
Absolute zero is the coldest possible temperature: -273.15°C, -459.67°F, or 0 Kelvin. The calculator will not accept anything lower.
