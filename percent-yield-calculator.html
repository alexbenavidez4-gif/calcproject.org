The formula

Percent yield = actual yield ÷ theoretical yield × 100.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Actual yield: 8.2; Theoretical yield: 10), the result is Percent yield 82.00%.

Good to know

Yields above 100 percent usually mean the product is wet or impure.
