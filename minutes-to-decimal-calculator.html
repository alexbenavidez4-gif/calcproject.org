How it works, chart and FAQ

The formula: decimal hours = minutes ÷ 60. There are 60 minutes in an hour, so a minute is 1/60 of an hour. Timesheets and payroll round the result to two decimal places.

Hours and minutes together: convert only the minutes, then add the whole hours. 7 hours 45 minutes is 7 + 45/60 = 7.75. 8 hours 20 minutes is 8 + 20/60 = 8.33. 37 hours 30 minutes is 37.5.

In Excel or Google Sheets: for a time like 7:45 in cell A1, use =A1*24 and format the cell as a number to get 7.75. Or use =HOUR(A1)+MINUTE(A1)/60. For minutes only, use =A1/60. To go back, =A1/24 formatted as [h]:mm turns 7.75 into 7:45.

Latitude and longitude: degrees and minutes work the same way. Decimal degrees = degrees + minutes/60 + seconds/3600, so 40° 26′ is 40.4333°.

How do you convert minutes to decimal?
Divide the minutes by 60. 15 minutes is 0.25 hours, 30 minutes is 0.5, and 45 minutes is 0.75. For hours and minutes together, convert the minutes and add the whole hours: 7 hours 45 minutes is 7 + 0.75 = 7.75 hours.

What is 20 minutes in decimal?
20 minutes is 0.33 hours (20 divided by 60 is 0.3333, rounded to two decimals). Payroll systems usually round to two decimals, so 20 minutes is entered as 0.33.

What is 45 minutes in decimal?
45 minutes is exactly 0.75 hours, because 45 divided by 60 is 0.75. 40 minutes is 0.67 hours and 10 minutes is 0.17 hours.

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal in Excel or Google Sheets?
If the cell holds a time such as 7:45, multiply it by 24 and format the result as a number: =A1*24 gives 7.75. You can also use =HOUR(A1)+MINUTE(A1)/60. If the cell holds only minutes, use =A1/60.

How do I convert decimal hours back to hours and minutes?
Keep the whole number as the hours and multiply the part after the decimal point by 60. For 7.75 hours, 0.75 x 60 = 45, so it is 7 hours 45 minutes. The calculator above does this when you choose decimal hours to hours and minutes.

Why do timesheets and payroll use decimal hours?
Pay is hours multiplied by an hourly rate, and that is easier with a decimal than with hours and minutes. 7.75 hours at $20 an hour is $155. Many time clocks round to two decimals, but your employer may round to the nearest tenth or quarter hour, so follow their policy.

How do I convert degrees and minutes to decimal degrees?
Divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the degrees. For latitude 40 degrees 26 minutes, 26 divided by 60 is 0.4333, so it is 40.4333 degrees. Add seconds divided by 3600 if you have seconds too.
