The formula

Kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237. One pound equals 0.45359237 kilograms.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Pounds: 10), the result is 10 lb = 4.5359 kg. It also shows 1 lb equals: 0.453592 kg; 1 kg equals: 2.2046 lb.
