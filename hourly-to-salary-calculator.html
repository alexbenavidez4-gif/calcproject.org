How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Hourly wage, Hours per week and Weeks worked per year.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Annual salary = hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Hourly wage: 25; Hours per week: 40; Weeks worked per year: 52), the result is Annual salary $52,000.00. It also shows Per month: $4,333.33; Every two weeks: $2,000.00; Per week: $1,000.00.

Good to know

Amounts are before taxes and deductions. Use fewer than 52 weeks if you have unpaid time off.
