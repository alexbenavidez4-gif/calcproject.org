How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Load, Distance from the pivot to the load and Distance from the pivot to your effort.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Effort × effort arm = load × load arm, so effort = load × load arm ÷ effort arm.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Load: 200; Distance from the pivot to the load: 1; Distance from the pivot to your effort: 4), the result is Effort needed 50. It also shows Mechanical advantage: 4.00x.

Good to know

The longer the handle side, the less effort you need.
