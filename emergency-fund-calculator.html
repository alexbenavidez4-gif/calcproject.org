How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Essential monthly expenses, Months to cover and Already saved.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Target = essential monthly expenses × months to cover.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Essential monthly expenses: 3500; Months to cover: 6; Already saved: 4000), the result is Emergency fund target $21,000.00. It also shows Still to save: $17,000.00; Saving $300 a month, months to finish: 57 months.

Good to know

Three to six months of essential expenses is the usual target. Count rent, food, utilities, insurance, and minimum debt payments.
