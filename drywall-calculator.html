How to use the drywall calculator

  1. Measure the room length, width, and wall height.
  2. Count the doors and windows so their area is subtracted.
  3. Enter the sheet size in square feet: 32 for 4x8, 40 for 4x10, 48 for 4x12.
  4. Add one or two extra sheets for cuts and damage.

The formula

Wall area minus door/window area, divided by sheet size.

Worked example

For example, a 12×10 ft room with 8 ft walls needs about 8 sheets of standard 4x8 drywall.

Good to know

  • Longer sheets mean fewer seams to tape. Use 4x12 if you can get them into the room.
  • Hang drywall horizontally on walls to cut the length of seams you finish.
  • For a ceiling, divide room length times width by the sheet size and add it to your wall count.

Common questions

How many sheets of drywall do I need for a 12x12 room?

A 12 × 12 ft room with 8 ft walls has 384 sq ft of wall. Without openings that is 12 sheets of 4x8. Add about 5 more for the ceiling.

How many square feet is a sheet of drywall?

A 4x8 sheet is 32 square feet, a 4x10 is 40, and a 4x12 is 48.

How much extra drywall should I buy?

Plan on 10 to 15 percent extra for cuts, breakage, and mistakes, more for rooms with many corners or angled ceilings.
