How to use the mulch calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the bed in feet.
  2. Enter the depth in inches. Two to 3 inches is right for most beds.
  3. Enter the bag size. Most bagged mulch is 2 cubic feet.
  4. Compare the bag count with the cubic yards for bulk delivery.

The formula

Volume = length × width × depth, then converted to bags or bulk units.

Worked example

For example, a 20×6 ft bed at 3 inches deep needs about 1.1 cubic yards — roughly 15 bags at 2 cubic ft each.

Good to know

  • Bulk mulch is usually cheaper once you need more than about 1.5 cubic yards (20 bags of 2 cu ft).
  • Keep mulch a few inches back from tree trunks and house siding.
  • For a round bed, multiply 3.14 by the radius squared for the area, then enter that as the length with a width of 1.

Common questions

How many bags of mulch are in a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so it takes 13.5 bags of 2 cubic foot mulch, or 9 bags of 3 cubic foot mulch.

How deep should mulch be?

Two to 3 inches for most beds. Go to 4 inches for coarse bark in areas with heavy weeds. Deeper than that can smother roots.

How much area does a cubic yard of mulch cover?

About 108 square feet at 3 inches deep, or 162 square feet at 2 inches deep.
