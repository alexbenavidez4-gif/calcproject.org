How to use the topsoil calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Enter the depth: 4 to 6 inches for a new lawn, 1/4 to 1/2 inch for top-dressing.
  3. Enter the bag size. Bagged topsoil is commonly 0.75 or 1 cubic foot.

The formula

Volume = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12, in cubic feet. Divide by 27 for cubic yards, or by the bag size for bags.

Worked example

A 20 × 10 ft area filled 4 inches deep needs 66.7 cubic feet, which is 2.47 cubic yards or 89 bags of 0.75 cubic foot topsoil.

Good to know

  • At this size, bulk delivery is far cheaper than bags. One cubic yard equals 36 bags of 0.75 cubic foot soil.
  • A cubic yard of topsoil weighs roughly 2,000 to 2,700 lb depending on moisture. Do not overload a pickup.
  • Soil settles after watering. Order about 10 percent extra.

Common questions

How much topsoil do I need for a new lawn?

Four to 6 inches of good topsoil. For 1,000 square feet at 4 inches that is about 12.3 cubic yards.

How many bags of topsoil are in a cubic yard?

Thirty-six bags of 0.75 cubic foot soil, 27 bags of 1 cubic foot, or 18 bags of 1.5 cubic feet.

How much does a cubic yard of topsoil cover?

About 81 square feet at 4 inches deep, 108 square feet at 3 inches, or 324 square feet at 1 inch.
