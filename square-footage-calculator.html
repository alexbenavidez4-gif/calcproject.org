How to use the square footage calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of each room at the floor, wall to wall.
  2. Enter each room on its own row. Tap Add another room for hallways and closets.
  3. For an L-shaped room, split it into two rectangles and enter each one as its own row.
  4. Use the total for flooring, or carry each room into the paint and trim calculators.

The formula

Each room's area = length × width; add every room together for the total, then divide by 9 for square yards.

Worked example

For example, a 12×10 ft room (120 sq ft) plus a 10×8 ft room (80 sq ft) adds up to 200 sq ft — about 22.2 square yards.

Good to know

  • Round each measurement up to the next inch before converting to feet. Short numbers cost you a trip back to the store.
  • To convert inches to feet, divide by 12. A wall that is 11 ft 6 in is 11.5 ft.
  • Closets, alcoves, and under-stair spaces count toward flooring even when they do not count toward listed living area.

Common questions

How do I calculate square footage?

Multiply the length of the room by its width, both in feet. A 12 ft by 10 ft room is 120 square feet. For several rooms, work out each one and add them together.

How do I convert square feet to square yards?

Divide square feet by 9. A 200 square foot area is about 22.2 square yards. Carpet is often priced by the square yard.

How do I measure a room that is not a rectangle?

Break it into rectangles, measure each one, and add the areas. For a triangle, multiply the two sides that meet at the square corner and divide by 2.
