The formula

Joists = deck length (in) ÷ joist spacing, rounded up, plus 1 for the end.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Deck length along the house: 16 ft; Joist spacing: 16 in), the result is Joists 13.

Good to know

  • Sixteen inches on center suits most wood decking. Composite decking and diagonal boards often need 12 inches. Joist size and span come from your local code.
  • At 12 inch spacing, a 16 ft deck needs 17 joists instead of 13.
  • Add rim joists and blocking separately. This counts the main joists only.

Common questions

How far apart should deck joists be?

Sixteen inches on center for most wood decking laid straight. Use 12 inches for composite boards, diagonal patterns, and decks with a hot tub.

How many joists do I need for a 16 foot deck?

At 16 inches on center, 16 ft takes 12 spaces plus 1, which is 13 joists.

What size joists should I use?

It depends on the span. As a rough guide at 16 inch spacing, 2x8 spans about 11 to 13 ft and 2x10 about 14 to 16 ft, but always check the span table in your local code.
