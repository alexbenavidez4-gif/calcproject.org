How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Vehicle price, Down payment, Trade-in value, Sales tax, Interest rate and Loan term.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Amount financed = price + sales tax − down payment − trade-in. Payment = L × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n) with a monthly rate r and n months.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Vehicle price: 32000; Down payment: 3000; Trade-in value: 5000; Sales tax: 6.25; Interest rate: 7; Loan term: 60), the result is Monthly payment $514.83. It also shows Sales tax: $2,000.00; Amount financed: $26,000.00; Total interest: $4,889.87.

Good to know

Some states tax only the price after the trade-in. Title, registration, and dealer fees are not included.
