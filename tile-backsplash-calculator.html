How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Backsplash length, Backsplash height, Tile length, Tile width and Waste allowance.
  2. Use the Imperial and Metric switch if you measure in meters and centimeters.
  3. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  4. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Area = length (ft) × height (in) ÷ 12. Tiles = area with waste × 144 ÷ (tile length × tile width).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Backsplash length: 10 ft; Backsplash height: 18 in; Tile length: 6 in; Tile width: 3 in; Waste allowance: 10), the result is Buy 132 tiles. It also shows Backsplash area: 15.0 sq ft; With waste: 16.5 sq ft.

Good to know

  • Standard backsplash height between counter and cabinets is 18 inches. Subtract windows and add the area behind the range if it runs higher.
  • Do not subtract outlets and switches. The cut tiles around them use up as much tile as they save.
  • Plan the layout from the center of the main wall so the cut tiles at each end match.

Common questions

How much tile do I need for a backsplash?

Multiply the length in feet by the height in feet. Ten feet of counter with an 18 inch backsplash is 15 square feet, which takes 132 subway tiles of 3 × 6 inches with waste.

How many subway tiles are in a square foot?

Eight 3 × 6 inch tiles cover one square foot.

How high should a backsplash go?

The standard is the 18 inches between the countertop and the upper cabinets. Many people run it to the ceiling behind the range hood.
