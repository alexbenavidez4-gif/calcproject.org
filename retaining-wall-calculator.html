How to use the retaining wall calculator

  1. Measure the wall length along its face.
  2. Enter the height you want above ground.
  3. Enter the face size of your block: its length and height.
  4. Keep the buried base row on. The first course sits below grade to lock the wall in.

The formula

Blocks per row = wall length (in) ÷ block length, rounded up. Rows = wall height (in) ÷ block height, rounded up, plus one buried base row. Blocks = blocks per row × rows. Caps = one row.

Worked example

A 20 ft long, 3 ft tall wall with 16 × 6 inch blocks needs 15 blocks per row and 6 rows above ground plus 1 buried row: 105 blocks and 15 caps.

Good to know

  • Dig a trench and lay 4 to 6 inches of compacted gravel under the first row.
  • Backfill behind the wall with drainage gravel and a perforated pipe so water pressure does not push it over.
  • Tapered blocks used on curves need the cap count checked against the manufacturer chart.

Common questions

How many retaining wall blocks do I need?

Divide the wall length by the block length for blocks per row, divide the height by the block height for rows, and multiply. Add a buried base row.

How high can I build a retaining wall without a permit?

Many areas allow walls up to 3 or 4 feet without a permit or engineering, measured from the bottom of the footing. Rules vary, so check with your building department.

Do retaining walls need drainage?

Yes. Gravel backfill and a drain pipe behind the wall let water escape. Trapped water is the most common reason these walls fail.
