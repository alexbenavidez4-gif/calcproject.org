How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in First mass, Second mass and Distance between centers.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

F = G × m1 × m2 ÷ r², with G = 6.6743 × 10^-11 N·m²/kg².

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (First mass: 5.972e24; Second mass: 70; Distance between centers: 6371000), the result is Force 687.3981 N.

Good to know

The starting numbers are the Earth and a 70 kg person at its surface, which gives the person’s weight.
