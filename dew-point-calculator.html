The formula

The Magnus formula: γ = ln(RH ÷ 100) + 17.625 × T ÷ (243.04 + T), and dew point = 243.04 × γ ÷ (17.625 − γ), with T in °C.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Air temperature: 75; Relative humidity: 50), the result is Dew point 55.1 °F. It also shows In Celsius: 12.8 °C.

Good to know

Dew points above about 65 °F feel muggy. Surfaces colder than the dew point collect condensation.
