The formula

Gallons = ceiling area (length × width) × coats ÷ coverage per gallon, rounded up to the next quart.

Good to know

  • Textured and popcorn ceilings can use twice as much paint. Drop the coverage to about 200 sq ft per gallon.
  • Use a flat finish. It hides roller marks and surface flaws better than anything shinier.
  • Paint the ceiling before the walls so drips and cut-in lines get covered.

Common questions

How much paint do I need for a 12x12 ceiling?

A 12 × 12 ft ceiling is 144 square feet. Two coats at 350 square feet per gallon take about 0.8 gallons, so buy 1 gallon.

Do ceilings need two coats?

Usually one coat is enough when repainting white over white with ceiling paint. Stains, new drywall, and color changes need primer or a second coat.

Is ceiling paint different from wall paint?

Yes. Ceiling paint is thicker so it spatters less, and it is very flat to hide imperfections.
