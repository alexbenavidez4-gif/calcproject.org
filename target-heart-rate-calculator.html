How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Age, Resting heart rate and Intensity.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Target = (maximum heart rate − resting heart rate) × intensity + resting heart rate, with maximum ≈ 220 − age.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Age: 40; Resting heart rate: 65; Intensity: 70), the result is Target heart rate 146 bpm. It also shows Maximum heart rate: 180 bpm; Heart rate reserve: 115 bpm.

Good to know

Moderate exercise is about 50 to 70 percent intensity and vigorous exercise is about 70 to 85 percent.
