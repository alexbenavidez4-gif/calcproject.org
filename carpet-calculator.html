How to use the carpet calculator

  1. Measure the room at its widest and longest points, into doorways.
  2. Add closets as part of the room if they get the same carpet.
  3. Use the same square yards for the carpet pad.

The formula

Square yards = room length (ft) × width (ft), plus waste, divided by 9.

Worked example

A 14 × 12 ft room is 168 sq ft. With 10 percent waste that is 184.8 sq ft, or 20.53 square yards of carpet and pad.

Good to know

  • Carpet comes in rolls 12 ft wide, sometimes 15 ft. A room wider than the roll needs a seam and more waste.
  • Patterned carpet needs extra length so the pattern lines up at seams.
  • For stairs, plan about 1 square yard for every 2 steps on a standard 3 ft wide stair.

Common questions

How do I convert square feet to square yards of carpet?

Divide the square feet by 9. A 180 square foot room is 20 square yards.

How much extra carpet should I order?

Add about 10 percent. Add more for patterned carpet or rooms wider than the roll.

How wide is a roll of carpet?

Most residential carpet is 12 feet wide. Some styles also come in 13.5 and 15 foot widths.
