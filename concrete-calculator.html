How to use the concrete calculator

  1. Measure the slab length and width in feet.
  2. Enter the thickness in inches. Four inches is standard for patios and walkways.
  3. Enter the yield of your bag in cubic feet: 0.60 for an 80 lb bag, 0.45 for 60 lb, 0.30 for 40 lb.
  4. Add 5 to 10 percent for spillage and uneven ground.

The formula

Volume = length × width × thickness, then ÷ bag size for bag count.

Worked example

For example, a 10×10 ft slab at 4 inches needs about 1.23 cubic yards — roughly 56 bags at 80lb each.

Source: Quikrete Concrete Mix data sheet (bag yields)

Good to know

  • Over about 1 cubic yard (45 bags of 80 lb), ready-mix delivery is usually easier than mixing by hand.
  • Driveways that carry vehicles are normally poured 5 to 6 inches thick.
  • Order a little extra. Running short in the middle of a pour leaves a weak cold joint.

Common questions

How many 80 lb bags of concrete make a cubic yard?

An 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 cubic feet, so it takes 45 bags to make one cubic yard (27 cubic feet).

How much concrete do I need for a 10x10 slab?

A 10 × 10 ft slab 4 inches thick is 33.3 cubic feet, or 1.23 cubic yards. That is 56 bags of 80 lb mix.

How thick should a concrete slab be?

Four inches for patios, walkways, and shed pads. Five to 6 inches for driveways and anywhere vehicles park.
