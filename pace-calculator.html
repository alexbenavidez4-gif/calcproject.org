How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Distance, Hours, Minutes and Seconds.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Pace = total time ÷ distance.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Distance: 3.1; Hours: 0; Minutes: 27; Seconds: 30), the result is Pace per mile 8:52. It also shows Pace per kilometer: 5:31; Speed: 6.76 mph.

Good to know

A 5K is 3.1 miles, a 10K is 6.2, a half marathon is 13.1, and a marathon is 26.2.
