How to use this converter

  1. Fill in Value, From and To.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Each unit is turned into a common base unit, then into the unit you want: result = value × (factor of the starting unit) ÷ (factor of the target unit).

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Value: 1; From: Gallons; To: Liters), the result is 1 gal = 3.7854 L. It also shows In mL: 3,785.41; In m³: 0.003785; In tsp: 768; In tbsp: 256; In fl oz: 128.

Good to know

Pick the unit you have under From and the unit you want under To. The other common units are listed above the answer.
