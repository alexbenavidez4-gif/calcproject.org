The Formula

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. For 180°C: 180 × 9 ÷ 5 = 324, and 324 + 32 = 356°F.
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: subtract 32, multiply by 5, then divide by 9. For 350°F: (350 − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 176.7°C.

Fan Ovens and Gas Ovens

A fan (convection) oven blows hot air around the food, so it cooks faster. If a recipe was written for a regular oven, lower the temperature by about 20°C, or about 25°F, or check the food earlier. Gas ovens use gas marks instead of degrees: gas mark 4 is 180°C, gas mark 6 is 200°C, and gas mark 7 is 220°C, as in the chart above.

Why “Double It and Add 30” Fails for Baking

A popular shortcut for converting Celsius to Fahrenheit is to double the number and add 30. It works for weather, where temperatures are small, but the error grows as the number gets bigger. At oven temperatures it is far off:

That is why this trick is fine for a 20°C day but not for a 180°C oven. For baking, use the chart or the exact formula.

Quick rule: 180°C is about 350°F, 200°C is about 400°F, and every 10°C is about 18°F. Home ovens are often off by 10 degrees or more, so an oven thermometer is worth having.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 180 C in Fahrenheit?
180 degrees Celsius is exactly 356 degrees Fahrenheit. Recipes and oven dials round it to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.

What is 200 C in Fahrenheit?
200 degrees Celsius is exactly 392 degrees Fahrenheit, usually set as 400 degrees Fahrenheit.

What is 350 F in Celsius?
350 degrees Fahrenheit is 176.7 degrees Celsius. Most Celsius ovens are set to 180 degrees.

What gas mark is 180 C?
180 degrees Celsius is gas mark 4, which is 350 degrees Fahrenheit.

Do I change the temperature for a fan oven?
Yes. Fan (convection) ovens move hot air around and cook faster, so a common rule is to lower the temperature in a recipe by about 20 degrees Celsius, or about 25 degrees Fahrenheit. Recipes usually say whether they were written for a fan oven, and your oven manual may have its own advice.

Is the Celsius to Fahrenheit conversion exact?
The formula is exact: multiply Celsius by 9/5 and add 32. Oven dials do not match every exact result, so recipes round to the nearest common setting, such as 356 degrees Fahrenheit to 350.
