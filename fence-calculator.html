How to use the fence calculator

  1. Measure the full fence line in feet, including every side.
  2. Enter the post spacing. Eight feet is standard for wood panels, 6 feet for many vinyl systems.
  3. Enter the panel width from the product label.
  4. Add posts for each gate and each corner beyond the basic count.

The formula

Posts = (fence length ÷ spacing) + 1, panels = fence length ÷ panel width.

Worked example

For example, 120 linear ft with 8 ft spacing needs 16 posts and 15 panels.

Good to know

  • Call 811 before you dig so buried utilities get marked.
  • Check your property line and any HOA or city height rules before you buy.
  • Each gate needs two posts of its own, usually heavier than line posts.

Common questions

How many fence posts do I need?

Divide the fence length by the post spacing, round up, and add one. A 120 ft fence with posts every 8 ft needs 16 posts.

How far apart should fence posts be?

Six to 8 feet for most wood and vinyl fences. Closer spacing makes a stronger fence in windy areas.

How deep should fence posts be set?

At least one third of the post length, and below the frost line in cold climates. A 6 ft fence typically needs holes 24 to 30 inches deep.
