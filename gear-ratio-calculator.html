How to use this calculator

  1. Fill in Teeth on the driven gear, Teeth on the driving gear and Input speed.
  2. The result updates as you type. Tap Calculate to run it again.
  3. Tap Save or print results to keep a copy or add it to your project sheet.

The formula

Gear ratio = teeth on the driven gear ÷ teeth on the driving gear. Output speed = input speed ÷ gear ratio.

Worked example

With the starting numbers on this page (Teeth on the driven gear: 40; Teeth on the driving gear: 10; Input speed: 1000), the result is Gear ratio 4 : 1. It also shows Output speed: 250.0 rpm; Torque multiplied by: 4.00x.

Good to know

A ratio above 1 trades speed for torque.
