How to use the roof pitch calculator

  1. Hold a level flat against the underside of a rafter or on the roof surface.
  2. Mark 12 inches along the level, then measure straight down from that mark to the roof. That is the rise.
  3. Enter the rise and a 12 inch run. Add the horizontal distance from wall to ridge for rafter length.

The formula

Pitch = rise ÷ run × 12, written as x/12. Angle = arctangent of rise ÷ run. The area multiplier is the square root of 1 + (rise ÷ run)², and rafter length = horizontal run × that multiplier.

Worked example

A roof that rises 6 inches over a 12 inch run is a 6/12 pitch, a 26.57 degree angle, and a 1.118 multiplier. Over a 12 ft run to the ridge, the rafter is about 13.42 ft before any overhang.

Good to know

  • Measuring from inside the attic along a rafter is safer than climbing on the roof.
  • Roofs at 4/12 to 9/12 are considered conventional. Below 2/12 needs low-slope roofing, not standard shingles.
  • Rafter length here stops at the outside wall line. Add your overhang separately.

Common questions

What does a 6/12 roof pitch mean?

The roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches it runs horizontally. That is an angle of about 26.6 degrees.

How do I convert roof pitch to degrees?

Divide the rise by the run and take the arctangent. A 4/12 pitch is 18.4 degrees, 8/12 is 33.7 degrees, and 12/12 is 45 degrees.

Why does pitch matter for buying shingles?

A steeper roof has more surface than its footprint. A 6/12 roof has about 11.8 percent more area than the flat footprint, and a 12/12 roof has 41.4 percent more.
